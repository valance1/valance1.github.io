Hi! I'm Gabriel Pinotti, a 2D and 3D artist from Brazil. I work mostly with 3D animation and 2D character drawing, and occasionally with video editing, VFX and programming.
I started making art when I was 12, animating stick figures and playing with Mine-imator. Later I took a technical course in informatics, which is where the programming side of my work comes from.
I'm 21 now and studying medicine at UFMG (Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais). Art and animation are still my main passion outside of class, and I keep making new projects whenever I can.
